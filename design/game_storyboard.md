## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**
Name: Hogwarts Heist
Theme: Through this game you will have to collect magical items to defeat Lord Voldemort and complete the game
before he finds you!

**Storyline:**
At Hogwarts school of witchcraft and wizardry you will be a student helping Harry Potter defeat Lord Voldemort. 
A powerful dark magic wizard who feared death above all else, to prevent it he put a part of his soul inside horcruxes which are
different objects in the castle. He believes in "Pure-Blood Supremacy" and wants to destroy all Muggles (non-magic) 
and Muggle-born wizards to rule the world. Help Harry find the horcruxes to defeat Lord Voldemort and preventing him 
from fulfilling his evil plan!

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Start room: The Great Hall
2. Room: Hogwarts Library & Restricted Section
3. Room: The Headmaster’s Office
4. Room: The Prefects' Bathroom
5. Room: Gryffindor Common Room
6. Room: Slytherin Common Room
7. Room: The Room of Requirement
8. Villain room: The Chamber of Secrets


## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Item: Tom Riddle's Diary
2. Item: Marvolo Gaunt's Ring
3. Item: Helga Hufflepuff's Cup
4. Item: Rowena Ravenclaw's Diadem
5. Item: Salazar Slytherin's Locket
6. Item: Nagini the snake

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

Lord Voldemort also known as Tom Riddle, became a powerful dark wizard who wants to destroy all muggles (non-magic),
muggle born witches and wizards and rule the world.
